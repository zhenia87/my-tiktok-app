Privacy Policy

Effective Date: July 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to VideoUpload ("we," "our," or "us"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our desktop application and website.

2. Information We Collect

We may collect information about you in a variety of ways. The information we may collect includes:

  • Authentication Data: When you connect your social media accounts via OAuth, we receive access tokens (e.g., user.info.basic, video.publish). We do not collect or store your passwords.
  • Usage Data: Basic technical logs generated locally on your machine regarding software performance and API upload statuses.

3. How We Use Your Information

Having accurate information about you permits us to provide you with a smooth, efficient, and customized experience. Specifically, we may use information collected about you via the application to:

  • Facilitate the uploading of video files to your linked accounts.
  • Generate performance reports and error logs for troubleshooting.
  • Comply with our legal obligations and platform API guidelines.

4. Disclosure of Your Information

We do NOT sell, trade, or otherwise transfer to outside parties your Personally Identifiable Information. All authentication tokens are stored locally on your device and are only transmitted directly to the respective official APIs (e.g., TikTok servers) for the sole purpose of publishing your content.

5. Data Security

We use administrative, technical, and physical security measures to help protect your personal information. Because VideoUpload is a local desktop application, the primary security of your data relies on the security of your own personal computer and operating system.
