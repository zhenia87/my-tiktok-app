Privacy Policy for TikTokUpload

Last Updated: May 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how TikTokUpload ("we", "our", or "the app") collects, uses, and protects your information.

1. Information We Collect

TikTokUpload requests access to basic user profile information and video publishing permissions (scopes: user.info.basic, video.publish) solely to authenticate you and publish your videos.

2. How We Use Information

We use the generated access tokens strictly to upload video files from our platform to your TikTok profile based on your direct requests.

3. Data Sharing

TikTokUpload does NOT sell, rent, or share your personal data or access tokens with any third parties.
